## Judging alignment

Innovation & Creativity, Experience Design, Technical Implementation, and Polish & Presentation each account for 25% of the official judging criteria. The central evidence should therefore show a distinct two-hand rain-guidance mechanic, clear seated interaction and onboarding, reliable WebXR behavior, and a finished three-level restoration arc. A polished entry page alone does not demonstrate these criteria.

The Technical Implementation criterion says high-scoring entries must perform on Meta Quest hardware at a minimum of **60 fps**. This is a scoring expectation, not evidence that this build meets it. Quest hardware frame rate, hand tracking, and comfort remain untested. The rules explicitly allow XR Simulator or equivalent emulator footage for the demonstration video; emulator footage does not verify hardware performance.

## Submission availability and fields

For IWSDK / WebXR, judges need a working application link; GitHub Pages is one supported hosting option, and another host may be used. The rules do not separately require a public source repository. The project and its access must remain free of charge and available through the Winner Announcement, currently expected around **2026-12-11**. After the Entry Period ends, do not change the submitted build or submission materials; judging uses their state at the deadline.

The submission must identify **Gaming / New Experience** and include a target launch date if the project has not been published to the Meta VR Store. The draft proposes 2026-11-10 as a planning date, leaving time before the competition deadline. This proposal is not an entrant-approved commitment; obtain confirmation or a revised date before final submission. Do not present the draft as submitted.

## Submission boundary

Per the working reading of §3, automated or proxy competition submission is invalid. Prepare the build, video, English copy, and checklist for the entrant; the entrant must personally perform the final competition submission. Start application submission and competition submission are separate actions.

Do not fabricate dates, membership, device test results, affiliations, or eligibility statements. Do not contact organizers or send messages without explicit user authorization.

## Originality and licensing

Do not reuse SupperShift implementation, branding, or submission artifacts. Public open-source code and development tools may be used according to their licenses and the official rules; record attribution and provenance. The resulting entry must be newly created during the competition period. Do not claim third-party work as original.

## Final audit

Before declaring the competition task complete, inspect the actual published experience, all required official form fields, public video duration/accessibility, English materials, licensing, Start approval, historical eligibility resolution, and evidence of the entrant's successful submission. Any missing requirement remains incomplete even if local builds and tests pass.
